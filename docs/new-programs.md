# New system programs

Ten programs to add as system templates. Each variation is one `templates` row; its exercises are `template_exercises` rows in the order listed, positions from 0. Exercise names are exact catalogue names where known. Sets and reps are written `sets × reps`, and `target_reps` is the text after the ×.

All new rows: `is_system = true`, `user_id = null`, `hide_from_onboarding = true`, `cover_image_url = null`. `program_order` as listed. `position` of variations from 1 in the order listed.

Blurbs contain no em dashes and no colons.

## 1. Upper Lower Push Pull Legs

- program_slug `upper-lower-push-pull-legs` · intermediate · gym · 5 days · order 17
- Blurb: Five days a week, each muscle trained twice. Two heavier days for upper and lower body, then push, pull and legs with more volume.

**Upper**
- Barbell Bench Press 4 × 6-8
- Bent Over Barbell Row 4 × 6-8
- Standing Military Press 3 × 8-10
- Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown 3 × 8-10
- Barbell Curl 2 × 10-12
- Triceps Pushdown 2 × 10-12

**Lower**
- Barbell Squat 4 × 6-8
- Romanian Deadlift 3 × 8-10
- Leg Press 3 × 10-12
- Lying Hamstring Curl 3 × 10-12
- Standing Calf Raises 3 × 12-15

**Push**

- Dumbbell Shoulder Press 3 × 8-10
- Dips - Chest Version 3 × 8-10
- Side Lateral Raise 3 × 12-15
- Triceps Overhead Extension with Rope 3 × 10-12

**Pull**
- Chin-Up 4 × 6-10
- Seated Cable Rows 4 × 8-10
- One-Arm Dumbbell Row 3 × 10-12 each
- Face Pull 3 × 12-15
- Incline Dumbbell Curl 3 × 10-12

**Legs**
- Front Barbell Squat 3 × 8-10
- Bulgarian Split Squat 3 × 8-10 each
- Leg Extensions 3 × 12-15
- Seated Hamstring Curl 3 × 12-15
- Seated Calf Raise 3 × 12-15

## 3. Volume, Light, Heavy

- program_slug `volume-light-heavy` · intermediate · gym · 3 days · order 19
- Blurb: The next step once 5x5 stops moving. Lots of work at a moderate weight early in the week, a light day in the middle, then one heavy set of 5 to beat last week.

**Volume Day**
- Barbell Squat 5 × 5
- Barbell Bench Press 5 × 5
- Barbell Deadlift 1 × 5

**Light Day**
- Barbell Squat 2 × 5
- Standing Military Press 3 × 5
- Chin-Up 3 × 8-10
- Hyperextensions (Back Extensions) 3 × 10-12

**Heavy Day**
- Barbell Squat 1 × 5
- Barbell Bench Press 1 × 5
- Power Clean 5 × 3

## 4. Tiered Strength

- program_slug `tiered-strength` · intermediate · gym · 4 days · order 20
- Blurb: Each day has one heavy lift, one lighter lift for more reps, and one easy accessory. On the heavy lift, push the last set for as many reps as you can.

**Day 1**

**Day 4**
